charity-check

Check a whole list of U.S. nonprofits against the IRS's own files: Publication 78, the automatic-revocation list, and optionally the Form 990-N e-Postcard file and the Business Master File. Free, and private: this page runs entirely in your browser and is not allowed to make network requests (see its Content-Security-Policy), so your list and the IRS files never leave this computer.

1. Your list

Columns named EIN, FEIN, TIN, Tax ID or Employer Identification Number are found automatically. 53-0196605, 530196605 and spreadsheet-mangled 530196605.0 or 12345678 all work. Save Excel sheets as CSV first.

2. IRS files (download them, then choose them here)

Your browser cannot fetch these for you, so download each one from the IRS (links below) and pick the saved file. Zip files can be used as they are. The IRS updates them monthly; see the IRS bulk data page.

3. Compare with an earlier check (optional)

Rows whose status, Publication 78 code, revocation or reinstatement date, 990-N termination or flags changed since then are listed first, and the CSV gets an irs_change column. Choose the same optional IRS files both times, or rows will look changed only because a file was left out.

Information from public IRS data, not legal or tax advice. IRS files lag by weeks; confirm large or unusual grants in IRS TEOS. Rev. Proc. 2011-33 describes when grantors may rely on Publication 78 or the Business Master File.
